Retro nonsense, correctly applied

CV in a Box

A physical CV packaged like old software. Deeply unnecessary, which is not the same thing as wrong. It is a small object lesson in presentation, memory, taste, and the useful engineering instinct of asking: what if we made the idea real?

Build video

Boxed software design.

A short video showing the design work behind the box. The premise is silly; the execution is deliberately not silly. That tension is basically the whole project.

Why

Because a PDF is obedient.

A normal CV says what you have done. This tries to show something as well: a fondness for old computers, a willingness to make a joke real, and enough practical follow-through to design, print, assemble and photograph the thing properly.

What it says

Memorable beats compliant.

The box is a small protest against everything looking the same. It still points at serious work: software engineering, Linux, Python, technical leadership, and the slightly dangerous claim that someone can be designed to maximise your team.
