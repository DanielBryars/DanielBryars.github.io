Belly Board

I won a competition for a day course to make a wooden belly board. Which is a pleasingly specific prize: part woodworking, part seaside engineering, part excuse to spend a day making something with a proper curve in it.

See the selected photos Open selected assets

The course

A day in a surf workshop.

The photos tell the useful version: timber on the bench, outline and shaping, maker's marks, then the finished form. I have kept the selection deliberately small so the page feels like a project, not a memory-card dump.

The object

Simple shape, real judgement.

A belly board is satisfyingly direct: wood, curve, edge, grain, water. It is a small project, but it still rewards the same things as bigger engineering work: material feel, sequence, proportion and not rushing the last few decisions.
